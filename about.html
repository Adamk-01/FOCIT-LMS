About Faculty of Computing & IT

Pioneering computational sciences, cyber defense, and software engineering education at the University of Osun State since 2008. Fully accredited by the National Universities Commission (NUC).

FACULTY PROFILE & HERITAGE

Shaping Leaders of Nigeria's Digital Economy

The Faculty of Computing and Information Technology (FOCIT) at the University of Osun State was established with a clear mandate: to produce high-caliber software architects, artificial intelligence practitioners, and cybersecurity specialists capable of competing at world-class standards.

Located on the Main Campus in Osogbo, the Faculty operates state-of-the-art computational facilities, maintains industry collaboration with top technology firms, and integrates rigorous theoretical computer science with hands-on systems engineering.

Vision

To be a premier center of computing excellence, innovation, and high-impact scientific discovery in West Africa.

Mission

To deliver rigorous computing curricula, foster applied research, and train graduates with high ethical standards.

STATE-OF-THE-ART FACILITIES

Computing Labs & Research Centers

Our students gain practical exposure within high-speed fiber networked laboratories equipped with specialized hardware and enterprise cloud tools.

Software Engineering Studio

Configured for collaborative agile sprints, code reviews, automated CI/CD pipelines, and microservice testing on containerized server clusters.

Cyber Defense & Forensics Lab

Isolated sandboxed network environment dedicated to vulnerability research, cryptographic analysis, penetration testing, and digital forensics.

AI & High-Performance Computing

GPU-accelerated workstation cluster supporting deep learning models, natural language processing for indigenous languages, and bio-computing simulations.
