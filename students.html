ACADEMIC STANDARDS

Official 5-Point Grading Scale

UNIOSUN operates on the standard National Universities Commission approved five-point Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) system.

Score Range (%) Letter Grade Grade Points Degree Classification (CGPA)
70 – 100% A 5.00 First Class Honours (4.50 – 5.00)
60 – 69% B 4.00 Second Class Upper (3.50 – 4.49)
50 – 59% C 3.00 Second Class Lower (2.40 – 3.49)
45 – 49% D 2.00 Third Class (1.50 – 2.39)
0 – 44% F (Fail) 0.00 Probation (CGPA < 1.00)

Credit Unit Regulations

Full-time students must register for a minimum of 15 credit units and a maximum of 24 credit units per semester.

Any overload beyond 24 units requires written recommendation from the Course Adviser and explicit approval by the Dean.

Attendance & Examination Eligibility

A minimum attendance of 75% in lectures, lab practicals, and tutorials is mandatory to qualify for semester examinations.

Biometric attendance records are synchronized directly with examination docket generation.
